Third-party software bundled in lib/
====================================

lib/sql-wasm.js, lib/sql-wasm.wasm, lib/sql-asm.js: sql.js 1.14.2 (https://github.com/sql-js/sql.js), MIT.
  sql.js is SQLite compiled to WebAssembly/asm.js with Emscripten. SQLite itself is in the public domain (https://sqlite.org/copyright.html).
lib/papaparse.min.js: Papa Parse 5.7.0 (https://www.papaparse.com), MIT.
lib/read-excel-file.min.js: read-excel-file 9.3.12 (https://gitlab.com/catamphetamine/read-excel-file), MIT.
  The bundle includes fflate 0.8.3 (MIT), saxen 11.2.0 (MIT) and worker-f 0.1.20 (MIT, per its package.json).
